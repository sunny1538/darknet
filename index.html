CircuitBazaar is a small, independently-run store selling development boards, sensors, modules and prototyping hardware for makers, students and embedded-systems hobbyists across India. We're based in Raipur, Chhattisgarh.

We started CircuitBazaar because sourcing reliable, well-documented components locally was harder than it should be. Every product we list ships with a datasheet or reference link — not guesswork.

Orders & pricing

  • All prices are listed in Indian Rupees (₹), inclusive of applicable taxes unless stated otherwise.
  • Placing an order reserves items against current stock. Until online payment is live, orders are marked pending payment until our team confirms it with you.
  • We reserve the right to cancel an order — with a full refund if payment was collected — if items are unavailable or a pricing error is found.

Payments & liability

  • Online payments, once enabled, are processed securely by our payment partner, Razorpay. We never store your card, UPI, or bank details.
  • Products are intended for prototyping, educational and hobbyist use. Always follow the datasheet and safety guidance for any component, especially those involving mains voltage.
  • We may update these terms at any time; continued use of the site means you accept the current version.

What we collect & why

  • Name, email, phone, shipping address and order details — collected when you check out or send a message, used only to process your order and respond to you.
  • If you tap "Use my current location," your device's coordinates are sent once to a map lookup service to pre-fill your address. We don't store raw location data — only the address you submit.
  • Order, buyer and message data is stored in our database, protected by rules that only let our signed-in admin team read or manage it — visitors can only submit new records, never read others'.

Payments, cookies & your rights

  • We never store your card, UPI, or bank details — payments are handled directly by Razorpay under its own security standards.
  • No third-party ad trackers are used; your cart lives only in your browser's memory for the current session.
  • You can ask us to access, correct, or delete your personal data any time — email sunny22101082@gmail.com.
